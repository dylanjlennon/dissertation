Increased resolution of food intake and the gut microbiome.

Most nutrition research records diet as fat, protein and carbohydrate, and reads the gut microbiome from a single stool sample. My dissertation built two tools to see both in finer detail: a microbiome map of the whole gut, and a molecular-level database of the foods people actually eat. This site lets you explore what they found, using the data reported in the dissertation.

Chapter 2 · Gut microbiome biogeography

A stool sample is one point at the end of a long tract

Six pairs of littermate piglets, one sampled at day of life 1 to 2 and one at day 9 to 10, were sampled at 13 points along the gut: three in each third of the small intestine and four along the large intestine. At every point I collected both the lumenal contents and the adherent mucosa, giving 26 sites per piglet. The last large-intestine site sits closest to what a stool sample captures.

Chapter 2 · Stool versus gut

How well does stool stand in for the rest of the gut?

For OTUs found both at a gut site and in stool, I regressed their abundance at the site on their abundance in stool. In the youngest piglets stool was a reasonable proxy almost everywhere. By day 9 to 10 it told you very little about the small intestine, and little about the large-intestine mucosa.

By day 10, stool explained 7% of the variation in small-intestine lumen abundances, down from 67% at day 1 to 2.The first ten days are when biogeography forms, and when stool starts to diverge from the gut it is meant to represent.
Chapter 3 · Foodomics

From a nutrition label to molecules

A Nutrition Facts label lists a handful of numbers. The USDA reference database lists dozens of molecules for each food, but not for the brand on the shelf. Foodomics links the two: it matches each brand-name food to USDA reference profiles, then adjusts the molecular profile to agree with the label, producing estimated fatty acids, amino acids and carbohydrate types for products whose composition was otherwise unknown. Profiles live in a versioned MySQL database, so a reformulated product keeps its history.

In a modeled ketogenic burrito meal, swapping two sets of equivalent brands with identical gram amounts doubled the carbohydrate dose. A year of simulated brand switching for that one meal produced eight distinct "foodomic spaces" in a principal coordinates analysis. Figures 3-13 to 3-16.

Chapter 4 · Foodomics in the clinic

The same diet ratio does not mean the same diet

Ketogenic therapy for epilepsy is prescribed as a ratio: grams of fat to grams of protein plus carbohydrate. I applied foodomics to the brand-specific intake records of children in UF's long-running precision ketogenic therapy cohort, people with seizures that had failed at least two medications, with records spanning up to 20 years.

Within every ratio stratum and every feeding route, the saturated, monounsaturated and polyunsaturated make-up of fat intake varied independently of the ratio.Patients on the same prescription can be eating very different fats. Foodomics makes that visible, and it opens the door to prescribing at the level of fatty acids. Figures 4-2 to 4-7.
Methods

How the analysis was done

Sequencing and processing

  • 16S rRNA V1 to V3, Illumina MiSeq
  • Quality filter Q > 30; reads joined with FLASH
  • QIIME 1.8 open-reference OTU picking, 97% (USEARCH), GreenGenes 13_8, PyNAST
  • Chimera removal with UCHIME
  • Run on UF HiPerGator

Normalization and filtering

  • Occupancy classification of OTUs (always high, switch, always low) as a biological filter
  • Site-level geometric-mean normalization (DESeq2 size factors) in place of rarefying, so no reads were discarded

Statistics, in R 3.3.1

  • Unweighted and weighted UniFrac, Bray-Curtis
  • PERMANOVA (vegan adonis) and PERMDISP, 999 permutations
  • PCoA in phyloseq
  • Kruskal-Wallis with Benjamini-Hochberg correction
  • Linear regression of site vs. stool abundances

Foodomics database

  • Nutrition Facts Labels collected via Qualtrics and audited
  • Linked to USDA SR28 seed profiles, then adjusted to label values
  • Persistent, versioned MySQL schema

Foodomic analysis

  • Ternary plots of macronutrient, fat saturation, PUFA and amino acid profiles
  • PCA and PCoA of molecular profiles
  • 365-day brand-switching simulation

Animal and human research

  • Piglet Neonatal ICU model (Lab Anim 2011), UF IACUC approved
  • Pediatric ketogenic therapy cohort, IRB approved, records 1995 to 2016
